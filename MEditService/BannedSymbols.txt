# The current time is read through the injected TimeProvider, never ambiently, so a fake clock
# can drive a test without a real wait.
P:System.DateTime.Now;Use the injected TimeProvider (GetLocalNow/GetUtcNow) instead of the ambient clock.
P:System.DateTime.UtcNow;Use the injected TimeProvider's GetUtcNow instead of the ambient clock.
P:System.DateTimeOffset.Now;Use the injected TimeProvider's GetLocalNow instead of the ambient clock.
P:System.DateTimeOffset.UtcNow;Use the injected TimeProvider's GetUtcNow instead of the ambient clock.
# A blocking wait on a Task's result is banned; await it instead.
P:System.Threading.Tasks.Task`1.Result;Await the task instead of blocking on Result.
