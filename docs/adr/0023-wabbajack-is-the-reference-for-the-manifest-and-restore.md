# Wabbajack is the reference for the manifest and restore

Where Wabbajack has an answer to what a record of a mod holds and how a mod is rebuilt from it, the manifest and restore adopt it, and every divergence and omission is recorded in [wabbajack.md](../out-of-scope/wabbajack.md). The rule governs the manifest and restore alone: mod management follows MO2 ([ADR-0017](0017-mo2-is-the-reference-for-mod-management.md)) and record editing xEdit ([ADR-0018](0018-xedit-is-the-reference-for-record-editing.md)).

## Alternatives rejected

- Wabbajack's compiler, shipped. Its licence permits it, but its stack serves distribution of one profile to strangers, and the register cuts most of it.
